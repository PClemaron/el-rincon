Rental Terms & House Rules

Vera Playa, Almería, Spain

Version 1.0 | Effective from 1 September 2026

El Rincon is also our own holiday home. We want you to enjoy your stay, but ask that you treat the apartment, its contents and our neighbours with care and respect.

1. Guests and occupancy

The apartment may be occupied by a maximum of 4 guests and only by the number of guests included in the booking.

Additional overnight guests are not permitted without our prior agreement.

Pets or other animals are not permitted unless agreed with us in advance. Where permission is given, an additional damage deposit may be required.

Spanish guest-registration requirements apply to stays at El Rincon. Before arrival, please complete the GuestAdmin registration form sent to you. This is required for statutory guest registration in Spain.

2. Looking after the apartment

Please:

  • use the protective towels provided on fabric furniture;
  • use mats to protect tables and other surfaces;
  • wash and put away kitchen equipment after use;
  • dispose of rubbish, food and hygiene waste before departure; and
  • leave the apartment reasonably clean and tidy.

The normal cleaning fee does not cover excessive cleaning or damage.

3. Arrival and departure

Check-in: 16:00 to 21:00
Check-out: before 11:00

Early check-in or late check-out may be available by prior arrangement, subject to availability and an additional charge. Please contact us in advance if you would like to request this.

Please follow the arrival, key collection and departure instructions sent to you before your stay.

4. No smoking

El Rincon is strictly non-smoking indoors.

Smoking is permitted outside only. Please use the ashtray provided.

5. Damage and breakages

Accidents happen. Please tell us or our Property Manager promptly if anything is broken or damaged.

Guests are responsible for the reasonable cost of repairing or replacing items lost or damaged through misuse, negligence or deliberate action, excluding normal wear and tear.

6. Keys and access devices

Please take care of all keys, electronic tags, fobs and remotes supplied during your stay.

  • Lost apartment keys or electric access tag: €100
  • Property Manager call-out for lockout or spare keys: €30
  • Additional locksmith, repair or replacement costs caused by lost or damaged keys, locks, fobs or remotes may also be charged.

7. Air-conditioning and equipment

Please keep doors and windows closed while the air-conditioning or heating is operating.

Do not disconnect, alter or interfere with television equipment, installed applications, electrical equipment or other equipment in the apartment.

If anything appears faulty or unsafe, stop using it and contact the Property Manager rather than attempting repairs yourself.

8. Security and parking

Please lock the apartment whenever you leave it and before going to bed.

One covered parking space, No. 12, is provided for the apartment. Please use only the allocated space.

Detailed access instructions, Wi-Fi information and contact details are provided privately to confirmed guests.

9. E-bike rental

Two e-bikes are available for guests to rent during their stay, subject to availability and prior arrangement.

Rental is charged at €25 for the first day and €5 for each additional day, per bike.

A refundable damage deposit of €100 per bike is required.

Guests are responsible for the reasonable cost of repairing or replacing an e-bike or associated equipment lost or damaged through misuse, negligence or deliberate action, excluding normal wear and tear. Any such costs may be deducted from the damage deposit, and any amount exceeding the deposit will also be payable by the guest.

Please contact us in advance if you would like to reserve one or both e-bikes.

10. Respect for neighbours, the urbanisation and naturist rules

El Rincon forms part of a naturist residential urbanisation. Please respect our neighbours and comply with the rules of the urbanisation.

Nudity is mandatory around the pool area only.

Parties, events, excessive noise and behaviour likely to cause nuisance or disturbance are not permitted.

11. Problems during your stay

Please report any problem with the apartment as soon as possible so that we or our Property Manager have an opportunity to resolve it.

Contact details are provided privately to confirmed guests.

12. Booking, payment and cancellation

The price, payment arrangements, deposit requirements and cancellation conditions are those stated when the booking is made.

Where a booking is made through a third-party booking platform, the payment and cancellation conditions applicable to that booking also apply.

13. Applicable law

Stays at El Rincon are subject to Spanish law and to any mandatory rights applicable to the guest.

These Terms may be made available in multiple languages. In the event of any discrepancy or inconsistency between the English version and any translated version, the English version will prevail, subject to any mandatory rights applicable to the guest.
